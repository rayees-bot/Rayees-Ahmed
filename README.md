# 💊 MediQuick – Every Medicine. One Platform.

### Universal Medicine Discovery and Delivery Platform

MediQuick is a medicine discovery and delivery application designed to help users find rare, unavailable, and hard-to-find medicines from nearby pharmacies through a single platform.

It connects customers with multiple medicine systems, including Allopathy, Ayurveda, Siddha, Homeopathy, and Unani, making medicine discovery and ordering easier and more convenient.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of MediQuick is to simplify medicine discovery by connecting users with nearby pharmacies, helping them locate rare medicines, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

People often struggle to find required medicines, especially rare and unavailable medicines, in nearby pharmacies. Visiting multiple medical shops wastes time and effort. There is a need for a single platform that provides medicine search, pharmacy availability, ordering, payment, and delivery tracking.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

MediQuick provides a centralized platform where users can:

- Search for medicines across different medical systems.
- Find nearby pharmacies.
- Discover rare and unavailable medicines.
- Order medicines online.
- Choose Cash on Delivery or online payment.
- Track delivery partners through GPS.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Universal Medicine Search
Search medicines from multiple medical systems:
- Allopathy
- Ayurveda
- Siddha
- Homeopathy
- Unani
- Herbal Products
- OTC Products

### 2. Medicine Hunter
Helps users find rare and hard-to-find medicines by checking connected pharmacy inventories.

### 3. Nearby Pharmacy Finder
Discover nearby pharmacies and view their locations, addresses, and medicine availability using interactive maps.

### 4. Medicine Ordering
- Add medicines to cart.
- Manage quantities.
- View order summary.
- Select delivery address.
- Place medicine orders.

### 5. Payment Management
Supports two payment methods:
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Online Payment

### 6. Real-Time Delivery Tracking
Track delivery partners on an interactive map and view delivery progress, location, and estimated arrival time.

### 7. Emergency Medicine Request
Submit requests for medicines that are unavailable and receive updates when connected pharmacies respond.

### 8. Notifications
Receive updates about medicine availability, order confirmation, payment status, and delivery progress.

## 🔄 Application Workflow

1. User registers or logs into MediQuick.
2. Searches for the required medicine.
3. Views available pharmacies and medicine details.
4. Adds medicines to the cart.
5. Selects a delivery address.
6. Chooses COD or online payment.
7. Places the order.
8. Pharmacy processes the order.
9. Delivery partner collects the medicine.
10. User tracks the delivery until it reaches their doorstep.
